隐私政策Privacy Policy

生效日期 2026-11-09，运营者 Hongxu DongEffective 2026-11-09. Operated by Hongxu Dong.

1

适用范围

本政策说明 Horosa 星阙插件在 ChatGPT 与 Codex 中使用时，位于 mcp.horosa.org 的计算服务处理哪些数据、为什么处理、交给谁、保留多久，以及你能怎样控制。它也适用于本网站 horosa.org 和你联系我们时提供的信息。运营者是个人开发者 Hongxu Dong，联系方式见 支持页。

Scope

This policy explains what data the calculation service at mcp.horosa.org handles when you use the Horosa 星阙 plugin in ChatGPT or Codex, why, who receives it, how long it is kept and what control you have. It also covers this website, horosa.org, and information you send when you contact us. The operator is Hongxu Dong, an individual developer; contact details are on the support page.

2

我们收到哪些数据

  • 计算输入：ChatGPT 按你的要求调用工具时，把你在对话中给出的内容填进工具参数发给服务。包括：出生或事件的日期、时间、时区；出生地或事件地点的坐标（城市级，服务收到后取整到 0.01°，约 1 公里）；合盘时两个人各自的出生资料；推运、返照等技法的目标日期与可选的目标地点；计算设置（如宫制、流派、命盘性别设置）；你给出的数字、卦码或随机种子；卜卦、六爻、小六壬、塔罗可选的问题文字；以及确认标记和可选的简短确认备注。每个工具具体收哪些字段，见文末附表。
  • 粗略位置提示：如果你在 ChatGPT 中允许共享位置，ChatGPT 会随调用附带它推断的粗略位置（城市、地区、国家、时区和粗略经纬度）。服务只在按「此时此地」起盘的工具中（附表中注明）取其中的经纬度、取整到 0.01° 后用于计算，其余字段不读取。出生地永远不取自位置提示，服务也从不索取你的精确位置。
  • ChatGPT 附带的元数据：ChatGPT 还会随每次调用附带由 OpenAI 生成的匿名用户 ID、匿名会话 ID、匿名组织 ID、语言区域和客户端类型提示。服务收到这些数据，但不读取、不使用、不保存。
  • 连接数据：ChatGPT 的调用由 OpenAI 的服务器发出，所以服务看不到你的 IP 地址；Codex 等客户端可能从你的设备直接连接，此时网络服务商 Cloudflare 会看到你的 IP 地址。服务进程不记录访问日志。
  • 联系我们时：你发邮件时我们会收到你的邮箱地址和邮件内容；在 GitHub 提交问题时，内容按 GitHub 的规则公开。
  • 访问本网站时：本站不使用 Cookie、统计或第三方脚本；托管方 GitHub Pages 会为安全和运维记录访问日志（如 IP 地址），我们收不到这些日志。

我们不要求姓名、联系方式、账号、精确位置、支付信息、证件号码或健康信息。请不要在问题文字或确认备注里写姓名、联系方式、健康状况等敏感信息：计算用不到它们。只有在得到对方同意时，才提供他人的出生资料。

Data we receive

  • Calculation inputs. When ChatGPT calls a tool at your request, it fills the tool's parameters with what you said in the conversation and sends them to the service. This covers the date, time and timezone of a birth or event; coordinates of the birthplace or event place (city level, rounded by the service to 0.01°, about 1 km); for relationship charts, each person's birth data; target dates and an optional target place for predictive techniques such as returns and progressions; calculation settings such as house system, school and the chart gender setting; numbers, hexagram codes or a random seed you give; optional question text for horary, Liu Yao, Xiao Liu Ren and tarot; and confirmation flags with an optional short confirmation note. The table at the end lists the exact fields of every tool.
  • Coarse location hint. If you allow location sharing in ChatGPT, ChatGPT attaches the coarse location it infers (city, region, country, timezone and approximate coordinates). Only for tools cast for "here and now" (marked in the table) does the service take the coordinates from that hint, round them to 0.01° and use them in the calculation; it does not read the other fields. A birthplace is never taken from the hint, and the service never asks for your precise location.
  • Metadata ChatGPT attaches. With every call ChatGPT also sends an anonymized user ID, an anonymized conversation ID and an anonymized organization ID generated by OpenAI, plus locale and client hints. The service receives them but does not read, use or keep them.
  • Connection data. ChatGPT's calls come from OpenAI's servers, so the service does not see your IP address. Clients such as Codex may connect from your device, in which case the network provider Cloudflare sees your IP address. The service keeps no access logs.
  • When you contact us. If you email us we receive your address and the message. Issues filed on GitHub are public under GitHub's rules.
  • When you visit this website. The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or third-party scripts. Its host, GitHub Pages, keeps access logs such as IP addresses for security and operations; we do not receive those logs.

We do not ask for names, contact details, accounts, precise location, payment details, government ID numbers or health information. Please keep names, contact details, health conditions and other sensitive details out of question text and notes: the calculation does not need them. Give another person's birth data only with their permission.

3

工具返回什么

每个计算工具返回同一种结构：引擎计算出的盘面分段文字、一张技法卡（所用设置、引擎与版本）、必要的提示，出错时返回错误码和需要向你确认的事项；参数确认工具只返回设置说明。结果可能复述你给的日期、时间、取整后的坐标和问题文字。公共版本会删去寿命、疾病、死亡类判读；结果里没有运行编号、追踪编号或请求时间戳。结果只返回给发出调用的 ChatGPT 或 Codex 会话，随后成为你对话的一部分，由 OpenAI 按其政策和你的设置保存。

What the tools return

Every calculation tool returns the same structure: the computed chart sections as text, a technique card (settings, engine and version used), any warnings, and on failure an error code with what needs confirming; the settings tool returns only an explanation of settings. Results may repeat the date, time, rounded coordinates and question text you gave. The public version removes lifespan, illness and death readings, and results contain no run IDs, trace IDs or request timestamps. Results go back only to the ChatGPT or Codex session that made the call, where they become part of your conversation and are kept by OpenAI under its policies and your settings.

4

我们如何使用

计算输入和位置提示只用于完成这一次计算并返回结果；邮件只用于回复你。我们不做画像，不跨请求关联数据，不用于广告、统计分析或训练 AI 模型，不出售、出租或交换任何数据。

How we use it

Calculation inputs and the location hint are used only to perform that one calculation and return the result; emails are used only to answer you. We do no profiling, do not link requests to each other, and do not use data for advertising, analytics or training AI models. We do not sell, rent or trade any data.

5

接收方

  • OpenAI（ChatGPT / Codex）：替你发出调用并接收结果。
  • Cloudflare：网络服务商，负责 mcp.horosa.org 的 HTTPS 连接，并把请求经加密隧道转发到我们的服务器，在传输中经手请求和结果。
  • GitHub：托管本网站，以及你提交的问题。
  • Google（Gmail）：支持邮箱的邮件服务商。

它们各自按自己的隐私政策处理所经手的数据。除此之外我们不向任何一方提供数据。如法律要求我们提供数据，我们能交出的只有当时仍持有的数据，计算服务本身不持有记录。

Recipients

  • OpenAI (ChatGPT / Codex), which makes calls on your behalf and receives the results.
  • Cloudflare, the network provider that serves the HTTPS connection for mcp.horosa.org and forwards requests through an encrypted tunnel to our server, handling requests and results in transit.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website and any issues you file.
  • Google (Gmail), the provider of the support mailbox.

Each handles the data that passes through it under its own privacy policy. We give data to no one else. If the law requires us to hand over data, we can only provide what we still hold at that time; the calculation service itself holds no records.

6

保留期限

  • 计算输入、位置提示和结果：只在服务器内存中处理，从不写入磁盘、数据库或日志。计算引擎会把算过的结果暂存在内存缓存中，服务每天自动重启一次并清空它，所以最长保留 24 小时。
  • ChatGPT 附带的元数据：不保存，请求处理完即丢弃。
  • Cloudflare 的连接数据：按 Cloudflare 的政策保留；我们不调取、不导出。
  • ChatGPT 中的对话：由 OpenAI 按其政策和你的设置保存。
  • 支持邮件：保留到问题处理完毕，最迟在最后一封往来邮件后 12 个月内删除；你可以要求提前删除。
  • GitHub 问题：公开保留，直到你或我们删除。
  • 网站访问日志：由 GitHub 按其政策保留，我们收不到。

Retention

  • Calculation inputs, location hint and results: processed only in the server's memory and never written to disk, a database or logs. The engine keeps computed results in an in-memory cache, which an automatic daily restart of the service clears, so they are kept for at most 24 hours.
  • Metadata ChatGPT attaches: not kept; discarded once the request is handled.
  • Cloudflare connection data: kept under Cloudflare's policy; we do not retrieve or export it.
  • Conversations in ChatGPT: kept by OpenAI under its policies and your settings.
  • Support emails: kept until the matter is resolved and deleted within 12 months of the last message; you can ask for earlier deletion.
  • GitHub issues: public until you or we delete them.
  • Website access logs: kept by GitHub under its policy; we do not receive them.

7

你的控制

  • 服务只收到你在对话中给出的内容。八字、紫微、六壬的出生地或提问地点可以不给（改用钟表时间）；问题文字可以留空。
  • 在 ChatGPT 的设置中关闭位置共享，服务就收不到位置提示：按此时此地起盘的工具会改用钟表时间，卜卦会说明需要位置并停止。
  • 可以随时在 ChatGPT 中停用或卸载本插件，也可以删除包含结果的对话。
  • 计算服务不保存可识别你的记录，没有账号和资料可供查询、更正或导出；支持邮件可以要求查询或删除。
  • 有任何隐私问题，请按 支持页 的方式联系我们。

Your controls

  • The service receives only what you give in the conversation. For BaZi, Zi Wei and Da Liu Ren you can leave out the birthplace or place of the question (clock time is used instead), and question text can be left empty.
  • Turn off location sharing in ChatGPT and the service receives no location hint: here-and-now tools fall back to clock time, and the horary tool says it needs a place and stops.
  • You can disable or uninstall the plugin in ChatGPT at any time, and delete conversations that contain results.
  • The calculation service keeps no records that identify you, so there is no account or profile to look up, correct or export; for support emails you can ask us to look up or delete them.
  • For any privacy question, contact us as described on the support page.

8

安全与处理地点

所有连接都使用 HTTPS。计算服务运行在美国境内由运营者管理的一台服务器上，容器只读、以非 root 身份运行，不对外开放端口，只经 Cloudflare 加密隧道接收请求。OpenAI、Cloudflare、GitHub、Google 可能在其他国家处理所经手的数据。

Security and where data is processed

All connections use HTTPS. The calculation service runs on a server the operator manages in the United States, in a read-only container as a non-root user, with no open inbound ports; requests arrive only through Cloudflare's encrypted tunnel. OpenAI, Cloudflare, GitHub and Google may process the data they handle in other countries.

未成年人

本服务面向 13 岁及以上用户，内容属传统文化与娱乐参考。我们不收集年龄信息，也不会有意收集 13 岁以下儿童的数据。

Minors

The service is for users aged 13 and over, and its content is a cultural and entertainment reference. We do not collect age information and do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
