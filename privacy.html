隐私政策Privacy Policy

生效日期 2026-11-06，运营者 Hongxu DongEffective 2026-11-06. Operated by Hongxu Dong.

1

我们处理哪些数据

当你通过 ChatGPT 或 Codex 使用 Horosa 星阙时，服务只收到完成一次计算所需的输入：出生或事件的日期、时间、时区，城市级精度的地点坐标（仅对需要地点的技法），以及可选的计算设置（如宫制、流派、性别设置、所问之事的简短文字）。服务不要求姓名、联系方式或账号，也不会索取你的当前位置；对「此时此地」类技法，客户端可能按你在 ChatGPT 中的设置提供粗略位置提示，服务只在计算中使用它。

Data we process

When you use Horosa 星阙 through ChatGPT or Codex, the service receives only what one calculation needs: the date, time and timezone of the birth or event, city-level coordinates for techniques that need a place, and optional calculation settings (house system, school, a gender setting, a short question text). It asks for no name, contact details or account and never requests your current location; for here-and-now techniques the client may pass a coarse location hint according to your ChatGPT settings, which the service uses only for that calculation.

2

我们如何使用

输入只用于即时计算并把结果返回给发出请求的客户端。我们不做画像、不做广告、不把数据出售或提供给第三方。

How we use it

Inputs are used only to compute the result and return it to the requesting client. No profiling, no advertising, no sale or sharing with third parties.

3

保留

服务不存储请求内容和结果：没有数据库，不写运行记录，不产生追踪日志；计算进程只在内存中暂存数据，进程结束即消失。服务进程不记录访问日志。网络代理服务商（Cloudflare）在转发请求时可能按其自身隐私政策短期保留连接元数据（如 IP 地址与时间）；我们不读取、不汇总这些记录。

Retention

The service stores neither requests nor results: no database, no run records, no trace logs; the calculation processes hold data in memory only while computing. The service processes keep no access logs. The network proxy provider (Cloudflare) may retain connection metadata such as IP address and time for a short period under its own privacy policy; we do not read or aggregate those records.

4

接收方

请求经 OpenAI（ChatGPT / Codex）与 Cloudflare 的基础设施到达我们的服务器；这些服务商各自的隐私政策适用于它们处理的数据。我们不向其他任何方传输数据。

Recipients

Requests reach our server through the infrastructure of OpenAI (ChatGPT / Codex) and Cloudflare; each provider's own privacy policy applies to the data it handles. We send data to no one else.

5

你的控制

因为我们不保留任何可识别你的数据，所以没有可供查询、更正或删除的记录。你可以随时在 ChatGPT 中停用或卸载本插件。

Your controls

Because we keep no data that identifies you, there are no records to look up, correct or delete. You can disable or uninstall the plugin in ChatGPT at any time.

6

未成年人

本服务面向 13 岁及以上用户，内容属传统文化与娱乐参考，不收集年龄信息。

Minors

The service is for users aged 13 and over. Its content is a cultural and entertainment reference. We do not collect age information.

7

变更与联系

本政策如有变更会在本页更新并标注生效日期。联系方式见 支持页。

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are posted on this page with a new effective date. See the support page for contact details.
