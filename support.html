反馈时请附上

反馈时请附上你给出的输入（日期、时间、时区、城市）与所用技法；不要附上他人的个人信息。我们不保存请求记录，所以无法从服务器端查到历史结果。

What to include

Please include the inputs you gave (date, time, timezone, city) and the technique used; do not include other people's personal data. We keep no request records, so past results cannot be retrieved server-side.
